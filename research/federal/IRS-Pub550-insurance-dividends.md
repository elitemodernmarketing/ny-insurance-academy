# IRS Publication 550 (2025), Investment Income and Expenses — insurance policy dividends

- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550
- Edition shown: Publication 550 (2025)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape, direct-quote extraction)

Verbatim:

"Dividends on insurance policies. Insurance policy dividends the insurer keeps and uses to pay your premiums are not taxable. However, you must report as taxable interest income the interest that is paid or credited on dividends left with the insurance company.

If dividends on an insurance contract (other than a modified endowment contract) are distributed to you, they are a partial return of the premiums you paid."

"Dividends on veterans' insurance. Dividends you receive on veterans' insurance policies are not taxable."
