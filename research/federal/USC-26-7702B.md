# 26 U.S.C. § 7702B — Treatment of qualified long-term care insurance (HIPAA 1996)

- Source URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/7702B
- Revision shown on source: amendments through Pub. L. 109–280 (2006); LII page cached 2026-11-17
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of Cornell LII U.S. Code)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "a qualified long-term care insurance contract shall be treated as an accident and health insurance contract," (a)(2) amounts received "shall be treated as amounts received for personal injuries and sickness and shall be treated as reimbursement for expenses actually incurred for medical care".
- (b)(1) A qualified LTC contract is one where "(A) the only insurance protection provided under such contract is coverage of qualified long-term care services, (B) such contract does not pay or reimburse expenses incurred for services or items to the extent that such expenses are reimbursable under title XVIII of the Social Security Act [Medicare] …, (C) such contract is guaranteed renewable, (D) such contract does not provide for a cash surrender value or other money that can be— (i) paid, assigned, or pledged as collateral for a loan, or (ii) borrowed …"
- (b)(2)(A) "A contract shall not fail to be described … by reason of payments being made on a per diem or other periodic basis without regard to the expenses incurred during the period to which the payments relate."
- (b)(2)(C) Refund rule does not apply "to any refund on the death of the insured, or on a complete surrender or cancellation of the contract, which cannot exceed the aggregate premiums paid under the contract."
- (c)(1) Qualified LTC services are services that "(A) are required by a chronically ill individual, and (B) are provided pursuant to a plan of care prescribed by a licensed health care practitioner."
- (c)(2)(A) "The term \"chronically ill individual\" means any individual who has been certified by a licensed health care practitioner as— (i) being unable to perform (without substantial assistance from another individual) at least 2 activities of daily living for a period of at least 90 days due to a loss of functional capacity, (ii) having a level of disability similar … or (iii) requiring substantial supervision to protect such individual from threats to health and safety due to severe cognitive impairment." Certification must be "within the preceding 12-month period".
- (c)(2)(B) "each of the following is an activity of daily living: (i) Eating. (ii) Toileting. (iii) Transferring. (iv) Bathing. (v) Dressing. (vi) Continence. A contract shall not be treated as a qualified long-term care insurance contract unless the determination … takes into account at least 5 of such activities."
- (e)(1) For LTC coverage provided "by a rider on or as part of a life insurance contract or an annuity contract … This title shall apply as if the portion of the contract providing such coverage is a separate contract."
- (g)(4)(B)(iii) Nonforfeiture provision offered must provide at least one of: "(I) Reduced paid-up insurance. (II) Extended term insurance. (III) Shortened benefit period. (IV) Other similar offerings approved by the appropriate State regulatory agency."
- (g)(2)(A)(i)(X) Model regulation "Section 12 (relating to requirement to offer inflation protection)" is among the consumer-protection requirements.
