# 29 U.S.C. § 1162 — COBRA continuation coverage (ERISA § 602)

- Source URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/1162
- Publisher: Legal Information Institute (Cornell) — U.S. Code
- Revision shown: credits "Pub. L. 93–406, title I, § 602, as added Pub. L. 99–272, title X, § 10002(a), Apr. 7, 1986 … Pub. L. 112–40, Oct. 21, 2011"
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape, statute-text tab only)

Verbatim excerpts:

- (1) Coverage "must consist of coverage which, as of the time the coverage is being provided, is identical to the
  coverage provided under the plan to similarly situated beneficiaries under the plan with respect to whom a qualifying
  event has not occurred."
- (2)(A)(i) "In the case of a qualifying event described in section 1163(2) of this title [termination (other than by
  reason of gross misconduct) or reduction of hours], … the date which is 18 months after the date of the qualifying event."
- (2)(A)(ii) If a second qualifying event occurs during the 18 months, "the date which is 36 months after the date of the
  qualifying event described in section 1163(2)".
- (2)(A)(iv) "In the case of a qualifying event not described in section 1163(2) or 1163(6) of this title, the date which
  is 36 months after the date of the qualifying event."
- (2)(A)(viii) disabled (under Social Security title II or XVI) during the first 60 days of continuation coverage: "any
  reference in clause (i) or (ii) to 18 months is deemed a reference to 29 months (with respect to all qualified
  beneficiaries)" if notice is given before the end of the 18 months.
- (2)(B) ends on "The date on which the employer ceases to provide any group health plan to any employee."
- (2)(C) ends on failure to pay premium; payment is timely "if made within 30 days after the date due".
- (2)(D) ends when the qualified beneficiary first becomes, after the election, "(i) covered under any other group health
  plan … which does not contain any exclusion or limitation with respect to any preexisting condition …, or (ii) …
  entitled to benefits under title XVIII of the Social Security Act [Medicare]."
- (3) premium "(A) shall not exceed 102 percent of the applicable premium for such period, and (B) may, at the election
  of the payor, be made in monthly installments. In no event may the plan require the payment of any premium before the
  day which is 45 days after the day on which the qualified beneficiary made the initial election … any reference … to
  '102 percent' is deemed a reference to '150 percent' for any month after the 18th month" for the disability extension.
- (4) "The coverage may not be conditioned upon, or discriminate on the basis of lack of, evidence of insurability."
- (5) when continuation expires, the plan must, during the 180-day period ending on the expiration date, offer
  "enrollment under a conversion health plan otherwise generally available under the plan."

Facts relied on: 18 months (termination/reduced hours); 36 months (other qualifying events such as death, divorce,
Medicare entitlement of the employee, child losing dependent status); 29 months (disability extension; premium up to
150% after month 18); premium up to 102%; 30-day premium grace; no evidence of insurability.
