# 42 U.S.C. § 300gg-3 — Prohibition of preexisting condition exclusions or other discrimination based on health status (PHSA § 2704; originally HIPAA)

- Source URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/300gg-3
- Publisher: Legal Information Institute (Cornell) — U.S. Code
- Revision shown: credits "§ 2704, formerly § 2701, as added Pub. L. 104–191 [HIPAA], title I, § 102(a), Aug. 21, 1996; … renumbered § 2704 and amended Pub. L. 111–148 [ACA], Mar. 23, 2010; … Pub. L. 112–40, Oct. 21, 2011"
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape, statute-text tab only)

Verbatim excerpts:

- (a) "A group health plan and a health insurance issuer offering group or individual health insurance coverage may not
  impose any preexisting condition exclusion with respect to such plan or coverage."
- (b)(1)(A) "The term 'preexisting condition exclusion' means, with respect to coverage, a limitation or exclusion of
  benefits relating to a condition based on the fact that the condition was present before the date of enrollment for
  such coverage, whether or not any medical advice, diagnosis, care, or treatment was recommended or received before such
  date."
- (b)(1)(B) "Genetic information shall not be treated as a condition … in the absence of a diagnosis of the condition
  related to such information."
- (b)(3) "late enrollee" = enrolls other than during "(A) the first period in which the individual is eligible to enroll
  under the plan, or (B) a special enrollment period".
- (b)(4) "The term 'waiting period' means, with respect to a group health plan and an individual who is a potential
  participant or beneficiary in the plan, the period that must pass with respect to the individual before the
  individual is eligible to be covered for benefits under the terms of the plan."
- (c)(2)(A) [HIPAA creditable coverage rule] creditable coverage not counted if followed by "a 63-day period during all
  of which the individual was not covered under any creditable coverage."
- (d)(3) "may not impose any preexisting condition exclusion relating to pregnancy as a preexisting condition."
- (f)(1) Special enrollment for individuals losing other coverage: employee who declined because of other coverage may
  enroll when that coverage (COBRA exhausted, loss of eligibility incl. "legal separation, divorce, death, termination of
  employment, or reduction in the number of hours of employment", or employer contributions ended) if the employee
  "requests such enrollment not later than 30 days after" the loss.
- (f)(2)(A)-(B) Dependent special enrollment when "a person becomes such a dependent of the individual through marriage,
  birth, or adoption or placement for adoption"; period "of not less than 30 days".
- (f)(2)(C) effective dates: marriage — "not later than the first day of the first month beginning after the date the
  completed request for enrollment is received"; birth — "as of the date of such birth"; adoption — "the date of such
  adoption or placement for adoption".
- (f)(3) Loss of Medicaid/CHIP or becoming eligible for premium assistance: request "not later than 60 days".
- (g) HMO "affiliation period" alternative to a preexisting condition exclusion: "does not exceed 2 months (or 3 months
  in the case of a late enrollee)" [historical HIPAA rule].

Facts relied on:
- ACA (amending HIPAA's section): no preexisting condition exclusions in group or individual health coverage.
- HIPAA special enrollment rights (30 days after losing other coverage or gaining a dependent by marriage/birth/adoption).
- Waiting period = time before an otherwise eligible person becomes eligible for benefits (probationary period).
