# IRS — IRC 457(b) deferred compensation plans

- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/irc-457b-deferred-compensation-plans
- Revision shown on source: not captured (Firecrawl cache 2026-11-17)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape, directQuote mode)

Verbatim excerpts:
- "Plans of deferred compensation described in IRC section 457 are available for certain state and local governments and non-governmental entities tax exempt under IRC Section 501. They can be either eligible plans under IRC 457(b) or ineligible plans under IRC 457(f). Plans eligible under 457(b) allow employees of sponsoring organizations to defer income taxation on retirement savings into future years."
- "The organization must be a state or local government or a tax-exempt organization under IRC 501(c)."
- "Contributions to a 457(b) plan are tax-deferred."
- "Earnings on the retirement money are tax-deferred."
