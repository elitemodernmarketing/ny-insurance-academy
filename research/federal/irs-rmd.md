# IRS — Retirement topics: Required minimum distributions (RMDs)

- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-required-minimum-distributions-rmds
- Revision shown on source: not captured (Firecrawl cache 2026-11-18)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape, directQuote mode)
- NOTE: the starting age (73) and excise-tax percentages are time-sensitive (changed by SECURE 2.0); content avoids them or flags them timeSensitive.

Verbatim excerpts:
- "Your RMD is the minimum amount you must withdraw from your account each year."
- "You can withdraw more than the minimum required amount."
- "The minimum distribution rules discussed below apply to original account holders and their beneficiaries in these types of plans: traditional IRAs, SEP IRAs, SIMPLE IRAs, 401(k) plans, 403(b) plans, 457(b) plans, profit sharing plans, other defined contribution plans, Roth IRA beneficiaries"
- "The required minimum distribution for any year is the account balance as of the end of the immediately preceding calendar year divided by a distribution period from the IRS's 'Uniform Lifetime Table.'"
- Required beginning date: "April 1 of the year following the calendar year in which you reach age 73."
- "If you don't take any distributions, or if the distributions are not large enough, you may have to pay a 25% excise tax on the amount not distributed as required (10% if withdrawn within 2 years)."
