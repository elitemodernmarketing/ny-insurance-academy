# IRS — SIMPLE IRA plan

- Source URL: https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-sponsor/simple-ira-plan
- Revision shown on source: not captured (Firecrawl cache 2026-11-19)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape, directQuote mode)

Verbatim excerpts (dollar limits intentionally not used by content):
- "A SIMPLE IRA plan (Savings Incentive Match PLan for Employees) allows employees and employers to contribute to traditional IRAs set up for employees. It is ideally suited as a start-up retirement savings plan for small employers not currently sponsoring a retirement plan."
- "Available to any small business – generally with 100 or fewer employees"
- "Employer cannot have any other retirement plan"
- "Employer is required to contribute each year either a: Matching contribution up to 3% of compensation … or 2% nonelective contribution for each eligible employee" (the page also notes a SECURE 2.0 4% match variation for some employers)
- "Employees may elect to contribute"
- "Employee is always 100% vested in (or, has ownership of) all SIMPLE IRA money"
