# Medicare.gov — Who pays first? (coordination of benefits / Medicare secondary payer)

- Source URL: https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/coordination/who-pays-first
- Revision shown on source: none shown (cached 2026-11-19). The page is an interactive question flow; its questions and answer texts are quoted below.
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content:

- "It depends on which insurance is considered \"primary\" and which is \"secondary.\" The insurance that pays first (primary payer) pays up to the limits of its coverage. The insurance that pays second (secondary payer) only pays if there are costs the primary insurance didn't cover."
- Flow questions: "How do you get your group health coverage? I have COBRA coverage / I'm covered by my former employer or my spouse's former employer (retiree coverage) / I'm covered by my current employer (including self-employment) or my spouse or family member's current employer"; "Does the employer providing your health insurance coverage have 20 or more employees (or is the employer part of a multi-employer group health plan where at least one company has 20 or more employees)?"; "Do you have Medicare because of a disability? I have ALS … I have End Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) … I get disability benefits for another reason"; "Have you received disability benefits for 24 months?"; "Does the employer providing your health insurance coverage have 100 or more employees …?"
- COBRA answer: "If you have Medicare because you're 65 or older, Medicare pays first."
- Retiree answer: "Medicare pays first, and your (or your spouse's) group health plan pays second."
- Current-employment answers: "The group health plan pays first, and Medicare pays second." / "Medicare pays first, and the group health plan pays second."
- Disability answers: "The large group health plan pays first, and Medicare pays second." / "Medicare pays first, and the large group health plan pays second. Note: When an employer has 100 or more employees, the health plan it offers is called a \"large group health plan.\""
- Explicit employer-size statement (non-tribal plan section): "If you have non-tribal group health plan coverage through an employer who has 20 or more employees, the non-tribal group health plan pays first, and Medicare pays second. If you have non-tribal group health plan coverage through an employer who has fewer than 20 employees, Medicare pays first, and the non-tribal group health plan pays second."
- ESRD: "Learn how Medicare coverage works if you have End-Stage Renal Disease." (details not on this page)
- Workers' comp: "Workers' compensation pays first for items or services (including drugs) related to the workers' compensation claim."
- No-fault/liability: "Your no-fault insurance or liability insurance pays first and Medicare pays second for services related to the accident or injury."
- Medicaid: "Generally, Medicare pays first. Medicaid never pays first for services Medicare covers."
- "If you're in a Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) Plan or an employer Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) that pays first, and you get services outside the group health plan's network, it's possible that neither the plan nor Medicare will pay."
