# 11 NYCRR § 58.1 (Regulation 193, Part 58) — Rules relating to content of forms for Medicare supplement insurance

- Source URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/11-NYCRR-58.1
- Revision shown on source: Amended NY State Register November 27, 2019 (Vol. XLI, Issue 48), eff. 1/1/2020
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of Cornell LII State Regulations)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- Intro: "The following shall be applicable to Medicare supplement insurance as defined in section 52.11 of this Title … Such rules shall apply to all Medicare supplement and Medicare select policies and certificates."
- (a)(13) "The term Medicare eligible expenses shall mean expenses of the kinds covered by Medicare Parts A and B, to the extent recognized as reasonable and medically necessary by Medicare."
- (b)(1)(i) "Every Medicare supplement insurance policy must be guaranteed renewable. … the insured has the right to continue the Medicare supplement insurance in force by the timely payment of premiums and the issuer has no unilateral right to make any change in any provision of the policy or certificate while the insurance is in force except to: (a) change benefits designed to cover cost-sharing amounts under Medicare to coincide with any changes in the applicable Medicare deductible amount and copayment percentage factors; (b) amend the policy to meet minimum standards for Medicare supplement insurance; or (c) revise premium rates on a class basis."
- (b)(3)(i) "the only permissible preexisting condition limitations applicable to Medicare supplement insurance are ones which exclude coverage, for no more than six months after the effective date of coverage under the policy or certificate, for a condition for which medical advice was given or treatment was recommended by or received from a physician, within six months before the effective date of the coverage."
- (b)(3)(ii) "an issuer shall credit the time the person was previously covered under creditable coverage, including Medicare supplement insurance, Medicare select coverage and Medicare advantage plans, if the previous creditable coverage was continuous to a date not more than 63 days prior to the enrollment date of the new coverage."
- (b)(4) No Medicare supplement policy may contain "limitations or exclusions on coverage that are more restrictive than those of Medicare" (except permitted preexisting condition limitations).
- (b)(11) Policies must state "the right to return the policy or certificate within 30 days of its delivery … and to receive a full refund of any premium paid therefor including any policy fees or other charges."
- (c)(1) "An issuer shall not cancel or nonrenew a Medicare supplement insurance policy or certificate for any reason other than nonpayment of premium or material misrepresentation." (c)(2) "… shall not cancel or nonrenew … on the ground of health status of the insured."
- (c)(7)(i) Benefits and premiums "shall be suspended at the request of the policyholder … for the period (not to exceed 24 months) in which the policyholder … has applied for and is determined to be entitled to medical assistance under title XIX [Medicaid] … but only if the policyholder … notifies the issuer … within 90 days after the date the individual becomes entitled to such assistance."
- (c)(7)(iii) Suspension at request of a person entitled to Medicare by disability (42 U.S.C. 426(b)) who "is covered under a group health plan"; reinstitution if notice within 90 days of loss of group coverage.
- (d)(1) "Applications may not contain any questions dealing with the health or health history of the applicant and no physical examination may be requested."
- (d)(2) Application notice "that the sale of a Medicare supplement policy is prohibited where an individual has a Medicare supplement policy in force and does not desire to replace the existing policy or where the Medicare supplement policy would duplicate benefits to which the individual is entitled under a Medicare advantage plan."
- (d)(3) "All applications for Medicare supplement insurance shall include the right to apply for standardized Medicare supplement benefit plans \"A\" and \"B\" and either \"D\" or \"G\" … [and] the right for individuals who first become eligible for Medicare before January 1, 2020 based on age or disability to apply for standardized Medicare supplement benefit plans \"C\" or \"F\" …"
- (d)(4) "In recommending the purchase or replacement of any Medicare supplement policy or certificate, an agent shall make reasonable efforts to determine the appropriateness of a recommended purchase or replacement. The application … taken by an agent shall include … a statement signed by the agent as follows: \"I have reviewed the current health insurance coverage of the applicant and find that additional coverage of the type and amount applied for is appropriate for the applicant's needs.\""
- (d)(5) "Any sale of a Medicare supplement insurance policy or certificate that will provide an individual with more than one Medicare supplement policy or certificate or duplicate benefits to which an individual is entitled under a Medicare advantage plan is prohibited."
- (d)(6)(i) Application statements: "(a) You do not need more than one Medicare supplement policy or certificate." "(c) You may be eligible for benefits under Medicaid and may not need a Medicare supplement policy (certificate)." "(f) Counseling services may be available in your state …"
- (d)(10) "With regard to individuals who are eligible for Medicare by reason of age, in no event may an issuer solicit coverage or accept applications more than 90 days prior to the month in which an individual has his or her 65th birthday."
- (e)(1) "Upon determining that a sale of a Medicare supplement insurance or Medicare select policy or certificate will involve replacement of accident and health insurance (including Medicare supplement insurance, Medicare select or Medicare advantage coverage) … an issuer, other than a direct response issuer, or its agent, shall furnish the applicant, prior to issuance or delivery … a notice regarding replacement of coverage." Notice: "Your new policy (certificate) will provide thirty (30) days within which you may decide without cost whether you desire to keep the policy (certificate)." "Terminate your present coverage only if, after due consideration, you find that purchase of this Medicare supplement coverage is a wise decision."
- (e)(3) Replacing issuer's notice: "Duplicate coverage is unnecessary and you should terminate one of your existing coverages if more than one such plan is still in force."
- (f)(1) "An issuer may provide commission or other compensation to an agent … for the sale of a Medicare supplement policy or certificate only if the first year commission or other first year compensation is no more than 200 percent of the commission or other compensation paid for selling or servicing the policy or certificate in the second year or period." (f)(2) "The commission … in subsequent (renewal) years must be the same as that provided in the second year or period and must be provided for no fewer than five renewal years." (f)(3) In a replacement, no compensation "greater than the renewal compensation payable by the replacing issuer on renewal policies". (f)(4) "compensation includes pecuniary or non-pecuniary remuneration of any kind … including but not limited to bonuses, gifts, prizes, awards and finder's fees."
- (g)(1) Issuer shall "(i) Establish marketing procedures to assure that any comparison of policies by its agents or other producers will be fair and accurate. (ii) Establish marketing procedures to assure excessive insurance is not sold or issued. (iii) Display prominently … on the first page of the policy the following: \"Notice to buyer: This policy may not cover all of your medical expenses.\" (iv) Inquire and otherwise make every reasonable effort to identify whether a prospective applicant … already has accident and health insurance and the types and amount of any such insurance."
- (g)(2) Prohibited: "(i) Twisting. Knowingly making any misleading representation or incomplete or fraudulent comparison of any insurance policies or insurers for the purpose of inducing … any person to lapse, forfeit, surrender, terminate … any insurance policy or to take out a policy of insurance with another insurer. (ii) High pressure tactics. Employing any method of marketing having the effect of or tending to induce the purchase of insurance through force, fright, threat whether explicit or implied, or undue pressure … (iii) Cold lead advertising. Making use directly or indirectly of any method of marketing which fails to disclose in a conspicuous manner that a purpose of the method of marketing is solicitation of insurance and that contact will be made by an insurance agent or insurance company."
- (i)(1) Open enrollment: "An issuer shall not deny or condition the issuance or effectiveness of any Medicare supplement policy or certificate available for sale in this State, nor discriminate in the pricing of such a policy or certificate because of the health status, claims experience, receipt of health care, or medical condition of an applicant. Applicants must be accepted at all times throughout the year for any Medicare supplement insurance benefit plan available from an issuer." (i)(2) "The requirements of paragraph (1) … shall be applicable to applicants enrolled in Medicare whether enrolled by reason of age or by reason of disability." (i)(3) This does not prevent applying a permitted preexisting condition limitation (except for federally guaranteed-issue individuals under (i)(4)).
- (m) Notice of Medicare changes must "(3) be in outline form and in clear and simple terms … and (4) not contain or be accompanied by any solicitation."
