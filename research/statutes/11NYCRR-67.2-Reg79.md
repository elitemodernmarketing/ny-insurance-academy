# 11 NYCRR § 67.2 (Regulation 79) — Inspection requirement for private passenger automobiles

- Source URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/new-york/11-NYCRR-67.2
- Page notes: amended eff. 4/1/2015, 5/20/2015 and 6/18/2024 (LII / Legal Information Institute)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape)

Verbatim text used by the content:

- "Except where an insurer has waived its right to an inspection or deferred an inspection pursuant to section 67.4 of this Part, an insurer shall not issue a new insurance policy or modify an existing insurance policy providing automobile physical damage insurance for a new, additional or replacement private passenger automobile unless the insurer's authorized representative has inspected the automobile."

Note: the specific waiver/deferral circumstances (§ 67.4) were not retrieved; content says only that some inspections may be waived or deferred under the regulation.
