# NY Insurance Law § 1102 — Insurer's license required; issuance

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/1102
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-09-22
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl single-page scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation, directQuote extraction)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content:

- (a) "No person, firm, association, corporation or joint-stock company shall do an insurance business in this state unless authorized by a license in force pursuant to the provisions of this chapter, or exempted by the provisions of this chapter from such requirement."
- (b) "No corporation organized under any law of this state shall do an insurance business outside this state unless so authorized pursuant to the provisions of this chapter or exempted by the provisions of this chapter from such requirement."

Note: the statute calls the insurer's authorization a "license"; the 17-55 outline and industry usage call it a "certificate of authority" (§ 2101(a) and § 2117 also refer to insurers "licensed or authorized" in this state).
