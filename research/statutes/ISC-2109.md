# NY Insurance Law § 2109 — Agents and brokers; temporary license in case of death, service in armed forces or disability

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/2109
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-10-03
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a) "The superintendent may issue a temporary insurance agent's license, title insurance agent's license or insurance broker's license … without requiring the applicant to pass a written examination … in the following cases: (1) in the case of the death of a person who at the time of his death was a licensed … agent … or a licensed insurance broker: (A) to the executor or administrator of the estate of such deceased agent or broker; (B) to a surviving next of kin … where no administrator … has been appointed and no executor has qualified …; (C) to the surviving member or members of a firm or association …; or (D) to an officer or director of a corporation upon the death of the only officer or director who was qualified as a sub-licensee …; (2) to any person who may be designated by a person licensed … and who is absent because of service in any branch of the armed forces of the United States …; and (3) to the next of kin of a person who has become totally disabled and prevented from pursuing any of the duties of his or her occupation …"
- (b)(2) "No fee shall be charged for any such license or any renewal thereof, except that fees for the renewals of any license issued pursuant to paragraph two [military] … shall be one-half of the fees otherwise required …"
- (c) The license authorizes the holder "to renew the business of the deceased, absent or disabled insurance agent … expiring during the period in which such temporary license or licenses are in force, to collect premiums due and payable to the original licensee … and to perform such other acts … as are incidental to the continuance of the insurance business of such original licensee."
- (d) "A person eligible for such a temporary agent's license may be licensed only as an agent of the insurer or insurers which such original licensee was licensed to represent at the time of such death, entrance upon military or naval duty or disability."
- (e)(1) [Death] "the license or licenses may be issued for a term not exceeding ninety days from the death of such deceased, and the superintendent may in his discretion renew such license or licenses for an additional term or terms of ninety days each, not exceeding in the aggregate fifteen months." (e)(4) "No person or persons so licensed shall, by virtue of such license, be authorized to solicit, negotiate or sell new insurance."
- (f)(1) [Military] "the license or licenses may be issued for a term not exceeding six months, and the superintendent may in his discretion renew such license or licenses for an additional term or terms of six months each." (f)(2) "The term of any such license or renewal shall in no event extend beyond sixty days after the final discharge of such absent insurance producer from military or naval duty …" (f)(3) "No person so licensed shall solicit new business under such license."
- (g)(1) [Disability] "the license or licenses may be issued for a term not exceeding ninety days from the disability of such person, and the superintendent may in his discretion renew such license or licenses for an additional term or terms of ninety days each, not exceeding in the aggregate fifteen months." (g)(2) "No person or persons so licensed shall … be authorized to solicit, negotiate or sell new insurance."
- (h) Title insurance agent temporary license: term not to exceed 180 days (not relevant to Life/A&H content).
