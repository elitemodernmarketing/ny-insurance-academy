# NY Insurance Law § 2121 — Broker authorized to receive premium, when

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/2121
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-09-22
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim text (full section):

- (a) "Any insurer which delivers in this state to any insurance broker or any insured represented by such broker a contract of insurance pursuant to the application or request of such broker, acting for an insured other than himself, shall be deemed to have authorized such broker to receive on its behalf payment of any premium which is due on such contract at the time of its issuance or delivery or payment of any installment of such premium or any additional premium which becomes due or payable thereafter on such contract, provided such payment is received by such broker within ninety days after the due date of such premium or installment thereof or after the date of delivery of a statement by the insurer of such additional premium."
- (b) "An agent who represents an insured for the purpose of obtaining insurance pursuant to any plan authorized by articles fifty-three, fifty-four and fifty-five of this chapter shall be deemed to be a broker for the purposes of this section."

Note: the 17-54 outline cites §§ 2121 and 2128 under "Sharing commissions", but § 2121 itself concerns a broker's deemed authority to receive premium on the insurer's behalf. Commission sharing between brokers is addressed in § 2324(c); payment of commissions only to licensed persons in § 2102(e).
