# NY Insurance Law § 2123 — Misrepresentations, misleading statements and incomplete comparisons

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/2123
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2024-09-20
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "No agent or representative of any insurer or health maintenance organization authorized to transact life, accident or health insurance … insurance broker … or other person, firm, association or corporation, shall issue or circulate or cause or permit to be issued or circulated, any illustration, circular, statement or memorandum misrepresenting the terms, benefits or advantages of any policy or contract of life, accident or health insurance, any annuity contract or any health maintenance organization contract … or shall make any misleading estimate as to the dividends or share of surplus or additional amounts to be received in the future on such policy or contract, or shall make any false or misleading statement as to the dividends or share of surplus or additional amounts previously paid … or shall make any misleading representation, or any misrepresentation, as to the financial condition of any such insurer … or as to the legal reserve system upon which such insurer … operates."
- (a)(2) "No such person, firm, association or corporation shall make to any person or persons any incomplete comparison of any such policies or contracts of any insurer, insurers, or health maintenance organization, for the purpose of inducing, or tending to induce, such person or persons to lapse, forfeit or surrender any insurance policy or health maintenance organization contract." [This is the conduct commonly called "twisting"; the statute itself does not use that word.]
- (a)(3) "Any replacement of individual life insurance policies or individual annuity contracts … shall conform to standards promulgated by regulation by the superintendent. Such regulation shall: (A) specify what constitutes the replacement … and the proper disclosure and notification procedures …; (B) require notification of the proposed replacement to the insurer whose policies or contracts are intended to be replaced; (C) require the timely exchange of illustrative and cost information …; and (D) provide for a sixty-day period following issuance of the replacement policies or contracts during which the policy or contract owner may return the policies or contracts and reinstate the replaced policies or contracts."
- (b) "Any comparison … shall be deemed to be an incomplete comparison if it does not conform to all the requirements for comparisons established by regulation."
- (c) "… it shall not be presumed that the insured knew or knows of any of the provisions, terms or benefits contained in any insurance policy …"
- (d) A violator who "shall knowingly receive any compensation or commission for the solicitation, sale or negotiation of any insurance policy … induced by a violation of this section shall also be liable for a civil penalty in the amount received by such violator as compensation or commission, which penalty may be sued for and recovered … by any person induced to purchase … by such violation. In addition, such [violator] shall be liable for a civil penalty in the amount of any compensation or commission lost by any agent, representative or broker as a result of a violation of this section …"
- (e) Banks and persons selling insurance on bank premises must disclose in writing that insurance offered "(1) is not a deposit; (2) is not insured by the federal deposit insurance corporation …; and (3) is not guaranteed by the … banking institution." (Marked "NB Repealed September 10, 2029".)
