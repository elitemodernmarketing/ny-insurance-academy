# NY Insurance Law § 2134 — Change of address

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/2134
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-09-22
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim text:

- (a) "A licensee under this article shall inform the superintendent by a means acceptable to the superintendent of a change of address within thirty days of the change."
- (b) "In addition to the requirements of subsection (a) of this section, a nonresident insurance producer who changes his, her or its home state, or a resident insurance producer who changes his, her or its home state to another state, within thirty days of the date of change, shall provide certification of such change from the new home state. No fee or license application shall be required therefor."

Note: the 17-55 outline heading is "Change of address (all addresses, including email)" and also cites Regs 5, 6 and 7 (11 NYCRR 21.4, 22.3, 23.4); those regulation texts were not retrieved. The statute itself says "address" without listing which addresses.
