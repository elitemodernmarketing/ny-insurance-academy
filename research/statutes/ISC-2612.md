# NY Insurance Law § 2612 — Discrimination based on being a victim of domestic violence

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/2612
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2023-01-06
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a) "No individual, insurer or entity subject to the supervision of the superintendent shall solely because a person is or has been a victim of domestic violence: (1) refuse to issue or renew, deny or cancel any insurance policy or contract; (2) demand or require a greater premium or payment from any person; (3) designate domestic violence as a preexisting condition, for which coverage will be denied or reduced; (4) fix any lower rate or discriminate in the fees or commissions of agents or brokers for writing or renewing such a policy."
- (b) "The fact that a person is or has been a victim of domestic violence is not a permitted underwriting criterion."
- (d) The prohibitions "shall not preclude an insurer from taking any of the actions described in subsection (a) … so long as such insurer relies on underwriting criteria reasonably related to the physical or mental condition of a person, their property or claim history and the decision was based on sound underwriting and actuarial principles reasonably related to actual or anticipated loss experience. … The insurer shall notify the insured of its specific reason or reasons for such decision."
- (f) If a person covered by a policy issued to another person as policyholder delivers to the insurer, at its home office, a valid order of protection against the policyholder (or, for non-health insurers, a request to designate an alternative mailing address, telephone number or method of contact because disclosure could endanger the person), "the insurer shall be prohibited … from disclosing to the policyholder the address, telephone number or other method of contact for the insured … any personally identifying information of the insured … or from mailing, delivering, or otherwise providing claim related information to any mailing address, telephone number, or other method of contact other than as designated by the requesting person …"
