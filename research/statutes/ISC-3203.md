# NY Insurance Law § 3203 — Individual life insurance policies; standard provisions

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/3203
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-09-22
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "for policies in which the amount and frequency of premiums may vary, after payment of the first premium, the policyholder is entitled to a sixty-one day grace period, beginning on the day when the insurer determines that the policy's net cash surrender value is insufficient … For all other policies, after payment of the first premium, the policyholder is entitled to a thirty-one day grace period or of one month following any subsequent premium due date … During such grace period, the policy shall continue in full force;"
- (a)(2) If death occurs within the grace period the insurer "may deduct from the policy proceeds the portion of any unpaid premium applicable to the period ending with the last day of the policy month in which such death occurred".
- (a)(3) "the policy shall be incontestable after being in force during the life of the insured for a period of two years from its date of issue … except in each case for nonpayment of premiums or violation of policy conditions relating to service in the armed forces." Increases requiring evidence of insurability are incontestable two years from the effective date of each increase.
- (a)(4) "the policy, together with the application therefor if a copy of such application is attached to the policy when issued, shall constitute the entire contract between the parties".
- (a)(5) "if the age of the insured has been misstated, any amount payable or benefit accruing under the policy shall be such as the premium would have purchased at the correct age;"
- (a)(6) "the insurer shall annually ascertain and apportion any divisible surplus accruing on the policy;"
- (a)(8)(A) policy loans available "where three full years' premiums have been paid" (or after three years from issue for flexible-premium policies); (B) "the sole security for the loan shall be assignment or pledge of the policy"; (I) "this paragraph eight shall not apply to term insurance".
- (a)(10) "the policy shall be reinstated at any time within three years from the date of default, unless the cash surrender value has been exhausted or the period of extended insurance has expired, if the policyholder makes application, provides evidence of insurability, including good health, satisfactory to the insurer, pays all overdue premiums with interest at a rate not exceeding six per centum per annum compounded annually, and pays or reinstates any other policy indebtedness".
- (a)(11) free look: "upon surrender of the policy, together with a written request for cancellation, to the insurer during a period of not less than ten days nor more than thirty days from the date the policy was delivered to the policy owner, the insurer shall refund … any premium paid for the policy, including any policy fees or other charges … a policy sold by mail order must contain a provision permitting the policy owner a thirty day period for such surrender."
- (b)(1) permitted exclusions for cause of death: conditions in (c) [war]; "(B) suicide within two years from the date of issue of the policy; (C) aviation under conditions specified in the policy; (D) hazardous occupations specified in the policy, provided death occurs within two years from the date of issue of the policy." May also exclude death while resident in a specified foreign country.
- (b)(3) If an excluded death occurs, insurer pays the reserve (or, if in force not more than two years, gross premiums less dividends and indebtedness).
- (c)(1) War exclusions permitted (armed forces service; special hazards of service; war within two years of issue while outside the home area as a civilian).
- (f) "This section shall not apply to group life insurance."
