# NY Insurance Law § 3220 — Group life insurance policies; standard provisions

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/3220
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2014-09-22
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "the policy is incontestable after two years from its date of issue, except for nonpayment of premiums by the policyholder; and that no statement made by any person insured under the policy relating to his insurability shall be used in contesting the validity of the insurance … after such insurance has been in force prior to the contest for a period of two years during such person's lifetime and in no event unless it is in a written instrument signed by him".
- (a)(4) "the insurer will issue to the policyholder for delivery to the person whose life is insured under such policy a certificate containing a statement of the insurance protection to which he is entitled".
- (a)(5) Benefits "shall be payable to the beneficiary or beneficiaries designated by the insured … if there is no such designated or specified beneficiary … [the amount] shall be payable to the estate of the insured".
- (a)(6) If insurance on an employee or member "(A) ceases because of termination of (i) employment or of membership in the class or classes eligible for coverage under the policy, or (ii) the policy … such employee or such member shall be entitled to have issued to him by the insurer, without evidence of insurability, upon application made to the insurer within thirty-one days after such termination or reduction of insurance and payment of the premium applicable to the class of risk to which he belongs and to the form and amount of the policy at his then attained age, a policy of life insurance only, in any one of the forms customarily issued by such insurer, except term insurance, in an amount equal to the amount of his protection under such group insurance policy at the time of such termination or reduction, less any amount of life insurance remaining in force".
- (a)(6) "at the option of such employee or member, he shall be entitled to have issued to him … a policy of life insurance only, in any one of such forms, preceded by term insurance for a period of one year".
- (a)(6) "upon the death of any such employee or member during such thirty-one day period and before any such individual policy has become effective, the amount of insurance for which such employee or member was entitled to make application shall be payable as a death benefit by the insurer".
- (a)(6) "An individual conversion policy shall not exclude or restrict liability in the event of suicide of the insured after two years from the date that the insured became covered under the group policy."
- (a)(7) "The effective date of the converted policy or policies shall be the date of termination of the employee's or member's insurance under the group policy." Conversion is also available "(B) upon the death of the employee or member to the surviving spouse … (C) to a child upon his attaining the limiting age of coverage … and (D) upon the divorce or annulment of the marriage".
- (a)(8) The certificate holder "shall be notified of such privilege and its duration within fifteen days before or after the happening of the event, provided that if such notice be given more than fifteen days, but less than ninety days after the happening of such event, the time allowed for the exercise of such privilege of conversion shall be extended for forty-five days after the giving of such notice. If such notice be not given within ninety days after the happening of the event, the time allowed for the exercise of such conversion privilege shall expire at the end of such ninety days."
- (a)(9) "all new employees of the employer or all new members of the labor union or other association or group … or all new debtors of the vendor or creditor … in the groups or classes eligible for such insurance must be added to such groups or classes for which they are respectively eligible."
- (a)(10) For union/association groups (other than certain groups), a notice "that the premium for the renewable term as therein provided depends upon the attained ages of the members in the group and increases with advancing ages."
