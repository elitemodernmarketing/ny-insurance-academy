# NY Insurance Law § 3231 — Rating of individual and small group health insurance policies; approval of superintendent

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/3231
- Revision shown on source: "Viewing most recent revision (from 2023-10-27)" (page notes "There are 2 § 3231's"; this is the rating section)
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)
- Note: § 3231 applies to commercial insurers (Article 32); § 4317 is the parallel rule for Article 43 corporations.

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "No individual health insurance policy and no group health insurance policy covering between one and fifty employees or members of the group or between one and one hundred employees or members of the group for policies issued or renewed on or after January first, two thousand sixteen exclusive of spouses and dependents, hereinafter referred to as a small group, providing hospital and/or medical benefits, including medicare supplemental insurance, shall be issued in this state unless such policy is community rated and, notwithstanding any other provisions of law, the underwriting of such policy involves no more than the imposition of a pre-existing condition limitation if otherwise permitted by this article."
- (a)(2) Any individual and dependents, and any small group, "applying for individual health insurance coverage … or small group health insurance coverage … must be accepted at all times throughout the year for any hospital and/or medical coverage offered by the insurer to individuals or small groups in this state."
- (a)(3) "Once accepted for coverage, an individual or small group cannot be terminated by the insurer due to claims experience. Termination of an individual or small group shall be based only on one or more of the reasons set forth in subsection (g) of section three thousand two hundred sixteen or subsection (p) of section three thousand two hundred twenty-one of this article."
- (a)(4) "For the purposes of this section, \"community rated\" means a rating methodology in which the premium for all persons covered by a policy form is the same based on the experience of the entire pool of risks of all individuals or small groups covered by the insurer without regard to age, sex, health status, tobacco usage or occupation, excluding those individuals or small groups covered by medicare supplemental insurance."
- (b)(2) "An insurer shall establish separate community rates for individuals as opposed to small groups."
- (c)(1) "The superintendent shall permit the use of separate community rates for reasonable geographic regions, which may, in a given case, include a single county."
- (e)(1)(B) "The expected minimum loss ratio for a policy form subject to this section … other than a medicare supplemental insurance policy … shall not be less than eighty-two percent."
