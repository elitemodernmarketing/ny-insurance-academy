# NY Insurance Law § 3425 — Certain property/casualty insurance policies; cancellation and renewal provisions

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/3425
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2026-07-03
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1) "\"Covered policy\" means a contract of insurance, referred to in this section as \"automobile insurance\", issued or issued for delivery in this state, on a risk located or resident in this state, insuring against losses or liabilities arising out of the ownership, operation, or use of a motor vehicle, predominantly used for non-business purposes, when a natural person is the named insured … provided, however, that the use or operation of the motor vehicle by a transportation network company driver as a TNC vehicle in accordance with article forty-four-B of the vehicle and traffic law … shall not be included in determining whether the motor vehicle is being used predominantly for non-business purposes."
- (a)(5) "A covered policy shall not include a policy issued pursuant to any plan established under article fifty-three or fifty-four of this chapter …"
- (a)(8) "With respect to automobile insurance, \"required policy period\" means a period of one year from the date as of which a covered policy becomes effective after first issuance or voluntary renewal."
- (a)(10) "Payment to the insurer, or to an agent or broker authorized to receive such payment, shall be timely, if made within fifteen days after the mailing to the insured of a notice of cancellation for nonpayment of premium."
- (b) "During the first sixty days a covered policy is in effect, no notice of cancellation shall be issued or be effective unless it states or is accompanied by a statement of the specific reason or reasons for such cancellation."
- (c) "After a covered policy has been in effect for sixty days, or upon the effective date if the policy is a renewal, no notice of cancellation shall be issued to become effective unless … based on one or more of the following: (1) With respect to automobile insurance policies: (A) nonpayment of premium, provided, however, that a notice of cancellation on this ground shall inform the insured of the amount due; (B) suspension or revocation during the required policy period of the driver's license of the named insured or any other person who customarily operates an automobile insured under the policy … ; or (C) discovery of fraud or material mis-representation in obtaining the policy or in the presentation of a claim thereunder."
- (d)(1) "Unless the insurer, at least forty-five but not more than sixty days in advance of the end of the policy period, mails or delivers to the named insured … a written notice of its intention not to renew a covered policy, or to condition its renewal upon change of limits or elimination of any coverages, the named insured shall be entitled to renew the policy upon timely payment of the premium … The specific reason or reasons for nonrenewal or conditioned renewal shall be stated in or shall accompany the notice."
- (f)(1) auto nonrenewals/conditional renewals limited "for each calendar year to two percent of the total number of covered policies of the insurer in force at last year-end in each such insurer's rating territory …"
- (h)(1) "Proof of mailing of a notice … to the named insured at the address shown in the policy, shall be sufficient proof of the giving of notice …"
- (h)(3) a copy of every notice "shall be mailed, delivered or transmitted to the insured's authorized agent or broker within seven days of the time such notice is mailed to the named insured."
- (i) "No insurer shall refuse to issue or renew a covered policy solely on the ground of the advanced age of the applicant or insured."
- (s) "With respect to automobile insurance, no insurer shall refuse to issue or renew a covered individually-owned private passenger policy solely on the ground that the motor vehicle to be insured under such policy shall be used for volunteer firefighting."

Note: the number of days' advance notice of a mid-term auto CANCELLATION (20 days; 15 days for nonpayment) is stated in Veh & Traf Law § 313(1)(a), not in § 3425 (see research/statutes/VAT-313.md).
