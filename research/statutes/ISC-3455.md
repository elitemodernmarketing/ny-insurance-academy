# NY Insurance Law § 3455 — Transportation network company group insurance policies

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/3455
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2017-06-30
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(3) "\"Transportation network company group policy\" or \"group policy\" means a group policy … where the group policyholder is a transportation network company and the policy provides insurance to the transportation network company and to group members: (A) in accordance with the requirements of article forty-four-B of the vehicle and traffic law; … and (C) in satisfaction of the financial responsibility requirements set forth in section three thousand four hundred twenty of this article, subdivision four of section three hundred eleven of the vehicle and traffic law, article fifty-one of this chapter …"
- (a)(4) "\"Group member\" means a transportation network company driver as defined in article forty-four-B of the vehicle and traffic law." (a)(5) "\"Group policyholder\" means a transportation network company."
- (c)(1) "A transportation network company group policy shall provide coverage for a TNC vehicle in accordance with the requirements of article forty-four-B of the vehicle and traffic law."
- (c)(2) the group policy "may provide: (A) coverage for limits higher than the minimum limits … (B) supplementary uninsured/underinsured motorists insurance … (C) supplemental spousal liability insurance … (D) motor vehicle physical damage coverage …"
- (d) premium may be paid from funds contributed "wholly by the group policyholder; … wholly by the group members; or … jointly".
- (j) "A transportation network company group policy shall not be subject to section three thousand four hundred twenty-five or section three thousand four hundred twenty-six of this article …"; (j)(2)(A) insurer's cancellation of a group policy "shall not become effective until thirty days after the insurer mails or delivers written notice of cancellation to the group policyholder …"
- (l) "An insurer may issue a transportation network company group policy to a transportation network company, notwithstanding that it may be a condition of operating a vehicle on the transportation network company's digital network for the TNC driver to participate in such group policy."

Related (from ISC-3425.md): TNC use is not counted in deciding whether a personal auto is "predominantly used for non-business purposes" (§ 3425(a)(1)); and (from ISC-5103.md) only the insurer of the driver's personal owner's policy may exclude no-fault benefits while the vehicle is used by a TNC driver (§ 5103(b)(4)).
