# NY Insurance Law § 4223 — Standard nonforfeiture law for annuities

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/4223
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2023-03-10
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(1)(A) "upon cessation of payment of considerations under a contract, the company will grant a paid-up annuity benefit on a plan stipulated in the contract".
- (a)(1)(B) "upon full or partial surrender of the contract at the commencement of any annuity payments or prior thereto … the company will pay in lieu of any paid-up annuity benefit a cash surrender benefit … The company shall reserve the right to defer the payment of such cash surrender benefit for a period of six months after demand therefor with surrender of the contract."
- (a)(1)(E)(i) For contracts with cash surrender benefits, annuity benefits at commencement will not be less than those provided by applying "the greater of the cash surrender benefit or ninety-five percent of what the cash surrender benefit would be if there were no withdrawal charge" to purchase a single consideration immediate annuity offered by the company.
- (b)(1) "This section shall not apply to any: (A) Reinsurance. (B) Group annuity contract purchased in connection with one or more retirement plans … (C) Premium deposit fund. (D) Variable annuity. (E) Immediate annuity. (F) Deferred annuity contract or group annuity certificate after annuity payments have commenced. (G) Reversionary annuity."
- (c)(1) "For contracts that provide a cash surrender benefit prior to the commencement of annuity payments, the death benefit attributable to any account, other than an equity index account, shall not be less than the actual accumulation amount … and the death benefit attributable to an equity index account shall not be less than the value of the equity index account".
- (c)(2) "actual accumulation amount" = net considerations credited, minus premium taxes and premium charges, plus interest (not less than the minimum annual effective rate), additional amounts and dividends credited, minus "administrative charges (which shall not exceed fifty dollars per year per contract)", minus transfers, prior withdrawals/partial surrenders and indebtedness.
- (c)(2)(F) "the minimum annual effective rate of interest shall be the lesser of three percent and the following: (i) the five-year constant maturity treasury rate … (ii) reduced by one hundred twenty-five basis points; and (iii) where the resulting minimum guaranteed interest rate is not less than one percent. The minimum annual effective rate of interest at issue shall be specified in the contract."
- (c)(4)(D) The equity index formula "shall be based on: (i) a percentage change in an equity index; (ii) guaranteed factors, such as participation rates, margins, caps and floors that adjust the percentage change in the equity index".
- (e)(3)(A) Without a market-value adjustment and without an equity index account, "'withdrawal charge percentage' means a percentage not greater than ten percent less the premium charge percentage, if any". (e)(3)(B) For an equity index account, that percentage is "reduced by one percent for each year beginning after the third year the contract has been in force and further reduced to zero after the tenth year".
- (e)(4) With a market-value adjustment formula, "'withdrawal charge percentage' means a percentage not greater than seven percent reduced by one percent for each year the contract has been in force".
- (h) "If the contract fails at any time prior to the commencement of annuity payments to provide cash surrender benefits or to provide death benefits at least equal to the actual accumulation amount, it shall contain a statement in a prominent place that such benefits are not provided."
- (k)(1) "At least once in each contract year, the company shall mail to each holder of a contract subject to this section under which benefit payments have not yet commenced a statement … as to any paid-up annuity benefit … any cash surrender benefit and any death benefit".
- (l) Operative date: January 1, 1981 (for companies that did not elect an earlier date).
