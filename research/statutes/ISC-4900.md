# NY Insurance Law § 4900 — Definitions (Article 49: Utilization Review and External Appeal)

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/4900
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2026-06-05
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation, structured verbatim extraction)

Verbatim excerpts used by the med content:

- "\"Utilization review\" means the review to determine whether health care services that have been provided, are being
  provided or are proposed to be provided to a patient, whether undertaken prior to, concurrent with or subsequent to the
  delivery of such services are medically necessary."
- "\"Utilization review agent\" means any insurer subject to article thirty-two or forty-three of this chapter and any
  municipal cooperative health benefit plan certified pursuant to article forty-seven of this chapter performing
  utilization review and any independent utilization review agent performing utilization review under contract with
  such insurer or municipal cooperative health benefit plan."
- "\"Adverse determination\" means a determination by a utilization review agent that an admission, extension of stay, or
  other health care service, upon review based on the information provided, is not medically necessary."
- "For the purposes of this article none of the following shall be considered utilization review: (1) Denials based on
  failure to obtain health care services from a designated or approved health care provider as required under a
  contract; (2) Where any determination is rendered pursuant to subdivision three-a of section twenty-eight hundred
  seven-c of the public health law; (3) The review of the appropriateness of the application of a particular coding to a
  patient, including the assignment of diagnosis and procedure; (4) Any issues relating to the determination of the
  amount or extent of payment other than determinations to deny payment based on an adverse determination; and (5) Any
  determination of any coverage issues other than whether health care services are or were medically necessary."

Facts relied on:
- UR = review of medical necessity, before (prospective), during (concurrent) or after (retrospective) services.
- A denial because the member used a non-designated (out-of-network) provider is NOT utilization review.
- Coding review and payment-amount issues are not UR; an "adverse determination" = not medically necessary.
