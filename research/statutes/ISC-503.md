# NY Insurance Law § 503 — Certificates of insurance; enforcement (Article 5)

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/503
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2015-07-31
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim text (complete section):

- "If the superintendent finds, after notice and hearing, that any person, other than a governmental entity, has wilfully violated this article, then the superintendent may order the person to pay to the people of this state a penalty in a sum of one thousand dollars for the first violation and two thousand dollars for each subsequent violation."
