# NY Insurance Law § 5106 — Fair claims settlement (no-fault)

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ISC/5106
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2023-12-29
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a) "Payments of first party benefits and additional first party benefits shall be made as the loss is incurred. Such benefits are overdue if not paid within thirty days after the claimant supplies proof of the fact and amount of loss sustained. … All overdue payments shall bear interest at the rate of two percent per month. If a valid claim or portion was overdue, the claimant shall also be entitled to recover his attorney's reasonable fee …"
- (b) "Every insurer shall provide a claimant with the option of submitting any dispute involving the insurer's liability to pay first party benefits, or additional first party benefits, the amount thereof or any other matter … to arbitration pursuant to simplified procedures to be promulgated or approved by the superintendent."
- (d)(1) "… If there is no such agreement, then the first insurer to whom notice of claim is given shall be responsible for payment. … If there is no such insurer and the motor vehicle accident occurs in this state, then an applicant who is a qualified person as defined in article fifty-two of this chapter shall institute the claim against the motor vehicle accident indemnification corporation."
- (d)(2) "A group policy issued pursuant to section three thousand four hundred fifty-five … shall provide first party benefits when a dispute exists as to whether a driver was using or operating a motor vehicle in connection with a transportation network company … when loss, damage, injury, or death occurs."
