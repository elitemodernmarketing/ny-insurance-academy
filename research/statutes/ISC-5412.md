# NY Insurance Law § 5412 — Additional powers of the association (NYPIUA / FAIR Plan)

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (a)(2) "\"Market\" means a line, subline or classification (other than a classification delineated by geographic location) of property/casualty insurance … Provided, however, a \"market\" shall also include homeowners insurance as defined in subsection (h) of section five thousand four hundred one of this article."
- (b) "The association shall begin, or resume after any suspension, its insurance underwriting operations for any market only after the superintendent has determined after a hearing on a record that it is necessary, due to unavailability of meaningful coverage in a particular voluntary market, to activate the association to write coverage for such market. In making a determination of necessity … the superintendent may consider such factors as: the extent and nature of competition; size and significance of the coverage; availability of adequate limits of coverage; efficacy of any market assistance program administered by the superintendent including … actual placement of coverage through a voluntary market assistance program …; reinsurance availability; extent of consumer complaints …; extent of denials and restrictions of coverage; volume of cancellations and nonrenewals … If, after activating the association in regard to a particular market, the superintendent determines that ready availability of meaningful coverage in such voluntary market has been restored, the association shall thereupon suspend its underwriting in regard to such market."
- (f) "The superintendent may also activate the association for purposes of providing excess or umbrella coverages in connection with a market. Hazards that the superintendent determines are uninsurable shall be excluded from coverages which the association is required to furnish."
