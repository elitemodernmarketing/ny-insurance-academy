# NY Public Health Law § 268-b — Establishment of NY State of Health, The Official Health Plan Marketplace

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/PBH/268-B
- Revision shown on source: "Viewing most recent revision (from 2019-04-19)"
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)

Verbatim excerpts:

- "1. There is hereby established an office within the department of health to be known as the "NY State of Health, The
  official health plan Marketplace"."
- "2. The purpose of the Marketplace is to facilitate enrollment in health coverage and the purchase and sale of qualified
  health plans and other health plans certified by the Marketplace; enroll individuals in coverage for which they are
  eligible in accordance with federal and state law; enable eligible individuals to receive premium tax credits,
  cost-sharing reductions, and to access insurance affordability programs and other health insurance programs as
  determined by the commissioner; assist eligible small employers in selecting qualified health plans and/or other, or
  additional health plans certified by the Marketplace and to qualify for small employer tax credits in accordance with
  applicable law; and to carry out other functions set forth in this title."

Facts relied on: New York's official health plan Marketplace (state-based exchange) is NY State of Health, an office
within the NY Department of Health; it enrolls individuals, enables premium tax credits and cost-sharing reductions,
and assists eligible small employers.
