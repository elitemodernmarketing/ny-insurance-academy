# NY Vehicle & Traffic Law § 313 — Notice of termination (Article 6)

- Source URL: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/VAT/313
- Revision shown on source: most recent revision from 2025-11-14
- Retrieved/verified: 2026-11-19 (via Firecrawl scrape of NYSenate Open Legislation)
- IMPORTANT: subdivisions 2, 3 and 4 have two versions on the page: one marked "Effective until December 31, 2028" and a replacement "Effective December 31, 2028" (online verification system). Content relying on § 313(2) filing deadlines is marked timeSensitive with a review date before 2028-12-31.

Verbatim excerpts used by the content (quotes trimmed with "…"):

- (1)(a) "No contract of insurance for which a certificate of insurance has been filed with the commissioner shall be terminated by cancellation by the insurer until at least twenty days after mailing to the named insured at the address shown on the policy a notice of termination by regular mail, with a certificate of mailing … except where the cancellation is for non-payment of premium in which case fifteen days notice of cancellation by the insurer shall be sufficient, provided, however, if another insurance contract has been procured, such other insurance contract shall, as of its effective date and hour, terminate the insurance previously in effect …"
- (1)(a) "No contract of insurance … in which a natural person is the named insured and the motor vehicle is used predominantly for non-business purposes shall be non-renewed by an insurer unless at least forty-five, but not more than sixty days in advance of the renewal date the insurer mails or delivers to the named insured … a written notice of its intention not to renew."
- (1)(a) "Every notice or acknowledgement of termination for any cause whatsoever sent to the insured shall include … a statement that proof of financial security is required to be maintained continuously throughout the registration period and a notice … indicating the punitive effects of failure to maintain continuous proof of financial security …"
- (2)(a) [effective until 2028-12-31] "Upon the termination of an owner's policy of liability insurance, other than an owner's policy of liability insurance for a motorcycle, at the request of the insured or by cancellation by the insurer, the insurer shall file a notice of termination with reference to such policy … with the commissioner not later than thirty days following the effective date of such cancellation or other termination …"
- (2)(b) [effective until 2028-12-31] "Upon the issuance of an owner's policy of liability insurance the insurer shall file a notice or confirmation of issuance … not later than fourteen days following the effective date of such issuance, and not later than seven days following the effective date for policies issued after January first, two thousand one …"
- (3) "A cancellation or termination for which notice is required to be filed with the commissioner … shall not be effective with respect to persons other than the named insured and members of the insured's household until the insurer has filed a notice thereof with the commissioner or until another insurance policy covering the same risk has been procured …"
- (4) [effective until 2028-12-31] "the commissioner shall establish a pilot program to maintain an up-to-date insured vehicle identification database to assist in identifying uninsured motor vehicles … (c) … easily accessible to on-duty law enforcement personnel … for the purpose of verifying whether an operator maintains proper insurance coverage". [Effective 2028-12-31] replaced by "a system for the online verification of motor vehicle liability insurance".

Note: "IIES" (Insurance Information and Enforcement System) is the DMV's name for this electronic insurance-reporting database; the statute itself does not use the acronym.
