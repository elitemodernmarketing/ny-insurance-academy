Verbatim excerpt used by the content (line breaks of the statute's column layout joined):

- Subdivision 1, "Group 12. Work as: Barbers … Blacksmiths … Carpenters … Chauffeurs … Domestic workers, other than those employed on farms, employed by the same employer for a minimum of forty hours per week"

Used for: the NY homeowners endorsement for workers' compensation for certain residence employees (outline plho.9.8) —
New York's Workers' Compensation Law lists domestic workers (other than farm workers) employed by the same employer at
least forty hours per week among covered employments, which is why a NY household employer may need workers'
compensation coverage for a residence employee.
